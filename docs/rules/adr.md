# Architecture decision records

- Record important enduring choices about architecture, contracts or operations when future work needs the reasoning. Do not require an ADR for every task.
- Use `docs/adr/NNNN-description.md` (next unused four-digit number) and [the Korean template](../templates/adr.md). Add it to `docs/adr/README.md`.
- Explain context, alternatives, decision/reasons, consequences and related evidence. A plan describes this task; an ADR preserves the lasting tradeoff; an archive closes the issue.
- Do not invent a decision for the user. Existing approval of a specific decision may be recorded directly; open alternatives remain proposed until decided.
- A plan conflicting with an accepted ADR needs a separate user decision. Record a scoped exception or a superseding ADR and link both records. Preserve the old context/history rather than silently rewriting it as current policy.
- New ADRs and user-reviewed additions are Korean. Existing historical ADRs may remain in their original language.
